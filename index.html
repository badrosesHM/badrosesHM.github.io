2016–2018

Unlocking the Kallters

To find and unlock the Kallters, complete a multi-day progression path in the Valley of the Hidden Dinosaur.

The progression path

  1. Enter Dino Valley.Reach at least Level 15 and complete the questline where you help Nic Stoneground repair his crashed hot air balloon. This opens the Valley of the Hidden Dinosaur.
  2. Help Nic Stoneground.Complete several days of exploration and setup quests for Nic Stoneground and Professor Einstein.
  3. Discover the Icengate.After roughly three days of consistent questing in the valley, a quest leads you up the Hidden Road to the massive frozen Icengate.
  4. Meet the outer expedition.Outside the gate, you finally meet Nanook and Sedna, the representatives of the Kallters’ outer expedition.

The reputation grind

Once unlocked, the Kallters work primarily as a reputation faction. Build their trust from Neutral to Popular to access their exclusive items and Generation 1 Fjord horses.

The dailies

Gather unique valley resources such as call stones, ice, thistles, ice berries, ice chestnuts, and snow weasels.

The reward

Reach the required reputation tier to purchase the Generation 1 Fjord horses sold outside the Icengate.

Unlocking Epona

Meet all of the following conditions to trigger Herman’s final text message and begin the Epona entrance questline.

Level requirement

You must be at least Level 14. Because of the reputation work involved, many players naturally reach Level 16 before unlocking Epona.

Friendly reputation required

  • GED Jarlaheim Office with Ms. Drake
  • Jorvik Stables
  • The Goldspur Family
  • The Jarlasson Family

Key prerequisite questlines

  • The Harvest Counties BridgeRebuild the bridge connecting Moorland to the Harvest Counties.
  • The Jarlaheim Mayor storylineHelp Jack Goldspur resolve Jarlaheim’s mayoral problems.
  • The German Spies and GretchenFind Gretchen around Fort Pinta and Hollow Woods, then help solve her family’s problems.
  • The Summerhouse ScamComplete the Newcomer questline and help Helga’s family deal with Mr. Kembell’s summerhouse scam.
  • Dino Valley explorationTalk to Nic Stoneground and help establish his camp in the Valley of the Hidden Dinosaur.
  • Ms. Drake’s reportsComplete her daily GED work in Jarlaheim through “A Step Up in Your Career?”, where you deliver her report to Herman.

Unlocking the secret path

  1. The message.Herman sends an urgent text asking you to meet him at Jorvik Stables.
  2. Operation Grim Harvest.Herman reveals GED’s dangerous expansion plans and asks you to sneak past them.
  3. The compass and the gates.Help the Goldspur family secure a special compass that reveals the hidden trail.
  4. Graydew Mountains.Travel through the secret, trap-filled mountain path and emerge on the other side in Epona.
